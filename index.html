🔥 Welcome to the Arena, Tributes!

The odds may be ever in your favor, but will your food safety knowledge be? Welcome to Climate on the Plate: Food Safety Challenges in a Changing World, brought to you by BSFT 4-1N! Step into an arena where the challenges go beyond survival and where knowledge is your greatest weapon.

Join us on November 27, 2026 (Friday), from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, at the Bulwagang Bonifacio, 4th Floor, PUP-NALLRC Building, PUP Main Campus.

As the climate changes, so do the challenges surrounding our food systems. Get ready to explore the growing threats to food safety, discover ways to combat these challenges, and equip yourself with knowledge that could make all the difference. Because in this arena, food safety is not just a skill—it's a matter of survival! 🌱 🍽️

🎯 Are you ready to take on the challenge?
Secure your spot, gather your courage, and enter the arena! May your knowledge be sharp, your awareness stronger, and the odds of food safety be ever in your favor.
